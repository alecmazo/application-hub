Coach insights

  • Forward passing has tracked results most closely. In the 4 games where at least half of our forward passes were completed, the record was 2W–2D–0L; in the 3 games below half it was 0W–1D–2L. Veo counts a pass as forward if it goes within 45° of straight at goal.
  • Shot share (our attempts as a share of all attempts) was above 50% in 6 of 11 games (3W–2D–1L), and 50% or below in 5 (2W–1D–2L).
  • Against the 3 possession-based opponents (55%+ of the ball and 15+ strings of four or more passes), the record was 0W–1D–2L with 11 goals conceded; those opponents averaged 22 strings of 4+ passes to our 6. Against balanced or direct opponents with string data, it was 2W–2D–0L.
  • Our share of the 4+ pass strings ranged from 17% to 62% (Davis, 5–0).
  • Field tilt (approx.) for Davis: 51%. Our successful passes in the attacking third were 17% of our total against 15% for the opponent, so territory was close to level although the score was not.

Weighted turnovers v2 (zone-risk weights)

A lost pass costs more the closer it is to our goal, because the opponent then has the ball nearer theirs. Karun Singh’s open expected-threat grid gives the chance that possession in each zone leads to a goal. Averaged by third it is 0.0094 in a team’s own third, 0.0153 in the middle and 0.0537 in the attacking third. Veo does not record where each pass was lost, so each role is given an assumed split of its lost passes by third. The weight is the expected opponent threat at that split, scaled so a central midfielder is 1.00. v1 (the weights the existing game and trend reports still use) is shown for comparison.

Player card ratings

  • Every outfield player with at least 60 capped minutes forms the reference pool (16 players). Each input is turned into a squad percentile (ties count half).
  • Face stat = 40 + 50 × weighted percentile, so ratings run from about 41 to 89. They compare players within this squad only.
  • PAC = 30% season-best top speed + 30% average speed + 40% high-intensity runs/70; SHO = 50% attempts/70 + 50% goals/70; PAS = 40% pass % + 40% weighted turnovers/70 (v2) (lower is better) + 20% passes/70; DRI = 100% dribbles/70; DEF = 40% interceptions/70 + 40% tackles/70 + 20% loose-ball recoveries/70; PHY = 60% duels/70 + 40% high-intensity runs/70. Inputs are per 70 minutes except pass %, top speed and average speed. PAC is 30% season-best top speed (best tagged figure across all games), 30% average speed (minutes-weighted Veo Average speed from the games that recorded it) and 40% high-intensity runs per 70 minutes in the same window as the other stats. PHY is 60% duels per 70 and 40% high-intensity runs per 70.
  • Overall = a role-weighted average of the six face stats. CB: DEF 40, PAS 25, PHY 20, PAC 15. FB: DEF 30, PAS 25, PAC 20, PHY 15, DRI 10. DM: PAS 35, DEF 30, PHY 20, PAC 10, DRI 5. CM / hybrid: PAS 35, DEF 15, PHY 15, SHO 15, DRI 10, PAC 10. AM: PAS 30, SHO 25, DRI 20, PAC 15, PHY 10. W: PAC 25, DRI 25, SHO 20, PAS 20, PHY 10. ST: SHO 40, PAC 20, PHY 15, DRI 15, PAS 10.
  • Players under 60 minutes still get a card, rated against the same pool and marked Small sample. Goalkeepers get an overall once Veo records saves: PAC, PAS, DEF and PHY are squad percentiles against the outfield pool; SAV is saves/70 on the same 40–90 scale, with 0 saves/70 = 40 and 7 or more = 90 (a one-keeper percentile would always be 65). GK overall weights: SAV 40, PAS 20, DEF 20, PAC 10, PHY 10. The save rate uses only games with a Saves column. Veo does not give shots on target against, so this is activity, not a save percentage.
  • Jerseys Veo tagged by mistake are left out, as are games a player missed through injury.
